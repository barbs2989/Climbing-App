
==============================================================================
wa_point_success_south_side — Point Success via Success Cleaver  [mountaineering]

--- climbing_route sections ---
  1. Lower Success Cleaver
  2. Upper cleaver and crest to ~10,700 ft
  3. Snow gullies to the Kautz Cleaver and summit

--- approach, in full ---
From the Longmire Wilderness Information Center (2,762 ft), pick up the Wonderland Trail heading southwest. About 3 miles in, after climbing out of Longmire, the trail drops steeply (~400+ ft) to a log-bridge crossing of Kautz Creek — a braided glacial outwash channel whose crossing is periodically damaged or washed out by debris flows off the Kautz Glacier, so check current status at the Longmire WIC before you leave, since a failed crossing can force a long reroute. Beyond the creek the trail climbs by switchbacks through old-growth forest, fords Fisher's Hornpipe Creek, and traverses a ridge past Devil's Dream backcountry camp (~5 mi, reliable water) before breaking out of the trees into the subalpine meadows of Indian Henry's Hunting Ground at roughly 6.3–7 mi and 5,300 ft, marked by the 1915 patrol cabin and open views of the Tahoma/South Tahoma icefall. From Indian Henry's, leave the maintained Wonderland Trail and follow the Mirror Lakes spur/climbers' path west (several plank crossings over marshy meadow, then end of maintained tread), continuing cross-country onto the southwest and west slopes of Pyramid Peak between about 6,000 and 6,300 ft to reach Point 6631 just north of the peak. Running water is often available here from snowmelt puddles into early summer, and the rock-slab camp below Pyramid Peak at ~6,300 ft (with running water nearby) is another common first-night camp for a 2-day ascent. From Point 6631, drop briefly NNE to a saddle at about 6,400 ft — the start of Success Cleaver proper. The lower cleaver is a loose, choss-and-scree slog with short bursts of class 3 scrambling and real rockfall exposure from parties above; there's no single obvious line, so expect route-finding. Travel improves noticeably higher up onto firmer rock and (early season) continuous snow, following goat tracks. Many parties place a second high camp on the cleaver crest around 10,700 ft (snow platforms can be dug in), from which the route continues up the crest — including a knife-edge section that requires real scrambling once snow melts off it — before angling climber's-right up snow gullies that merge onto the Kautz Cleaver around 12,200–12,600 ft, then up steepening snow/ice (to roughly 45°, with an isolated ~15 ft step near 55° used to bypass a cliff band in some seasons) to Point Success at 14,158 ft. All-in this is roughly 12–13 miles and 8,000+ ft of gain from Longmire to Point Success, so most parties split it over 2 days with an intermediate camp rather than going car-to-car in a single push.
