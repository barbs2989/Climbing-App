# Snow on a normal summit day — review rule (owner, 2026-11-12)

The route's Conditions card warns "Freezing level above the summit all night", "Freezing level … above the
summit by afternoon — expect rock and ice fall" and "no overnight refreeze". Owner: these only matter for
routes that cross a glacier or snow **on a normal summit attempt day**. A normal day is one in the route's
**peak season** (e.g. late July–September in the Cascades/Sierra/Rockies; the dry-rock window the route's
own text names).

Each route gets `snowInSeason`:

- **yes** — on a normal peak-season day the party crosses or climbs snow, firn, névé or glacier: a
  permanent snowfield or glacier on the approach, climb or descent; a snow col, snow couloir or snow
  descent that holds snow all season; crampons/ice axe/pickets listed as required gear for the normal ascent
  (not "early season", not "optional").
- **no** — every snow mention is early-season, spring/winter, after a storm, "lingers into June/July/early
  summer", "until snow clears", optional gear, an avalanche *path* or debris as terrain, a place name
  (Snow Creek, Snow Lakes, Snowy Range), a road closure, or a neighbouring route/peak the party does not cross.
- **unsure** — the text cannot tell (e.g. "snowfield" at the base with no season given, "depending on the
  snow", "snow into August"). Unsure KEEPS the warnings, so use it whenever a missed snow crossing is possible.

Asymmetry: wrongly dropping the warning on a route that does cross snow is the worse error.

Only the route's own text (the sentences given, each tagged with its source column) is evidence.
No web search, no browser.
