What each area of the app does **now**, where its code lives, and what changed recently.
Architecture, commands and working rules are in [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md); this file does not repeat them.

database tables · connections to other areas · the guards that protect it · known gaps.

## Recent changes

Newest first. Keep about 15 lines; older entries live in the PRs and the commit log.
- **No Fire map on a country or state page**: the red *Fire map* button is no longer drawn on the header of a country or state page — the first screen after picking a country and state on the Climbs tab; it starts at the range, as the Save button does, and still opens from every range, peak, crag and wall page and a route's Safety tab (`lib/DbAreaBrowser.jsx`, `AreaPage`).
- **Feedback and Rate app actually work** (0365): Feedback sends your note to the ClimbMatch team (it only copied it before); Rate app records your stars and an optional comment (it recorded nothing and thanked you anyway). The owner reads both in Menu › Review › *Feedback & ratings*, with the average rating. When ClimbMatch gets an App Store or Google Play listing, setting `VITE_APP_STORE_ID` / `VITE_PLAY_STORE_ID` adds a *Rate us on …* button for that device, shown to everyone, never only to happy raters (store rules). About us shows the real version and Send feedback / Rate buttons. See `docs/features/profile-and-settings.md`.
- **Safety tab reordered**: the forecast opens it on every route, then active fires (now a dropdown), known hazards, cell coverage, emergency & rescue, and the float plan last (also a dropdown) — see `docs/features/route-page.md`.
- **Requests reads top to bottom**: everything waiting on you — including group invites and group join requests, which used to sit under *Waiting on others* — now comes before the requests you sent; friend requests are shown open (no *Tap to review* fold) with when each was sent; every Accept is the same green button. See `docs/features/logbook-crews-home.md`.
- **Every ← Back and ✕ has a blue edge**: the close ✕, the ← Back (in popups and in-page, over photos too) and the remove ✕ on tags, days, photos and list rows now carry the same blue ring as the résumé's Edit button, in place of the faint white one. One token in `lib/popupChrome.js`; the 18px ✕ inside a chip keeps its white ring.
- **No "Continue as a demo climber" on sign-in**: signing out of any build with a backend (not only the live site) lands on the real sign-in screen with *View as guest*; the old demo screen, now reached only by a build with no Supabase, offers *View as guest* too instead of a sample account. See `docs/features/profile-and-settings.md`.
- **Climate & season moved to the Conditions tab**: the CLIMATE & SEASON box (the typical weather, spring to winter, and the forecast zone) and its *No season guidance* note left the Plan tab (and a crag route's Overview) for the Conditions tab, after the forecast, beside ASPECT & SUN and SEASONAL GUIDANCE. A discipline with no Conditions tab (aid) keeps it where it was. `check:bare` holds it: once, on Conditions.
- **Each rappel is a dropdown**: under RAPPELS a station shows its number, length and anchor (and a red hazard count when it has any); its station, hazards, pull and notes open with *▸ More*, the way a camp opens under CAMPING & BIVY. The first opens expanded (`RappelRow`).
- **Home: Finish setting up no longer flashes on a set-up account**: a climber with nothing left to set up saw the card pop up for a moment on each load, asking them to verify an email they had verified (or save a first objective they had saved), then vanish. The card waited for those reads to land but checked the state the app copies from them a render later; it now checks the rows themselves.
- **Forecast links open the peak's own page**: *Mountain-Forecast.com* under Weather & mountain forecasts opened a Google search; it now lands straight on the nearest peak's forecast (Mount Rainier → Mount Rainier), and *Fire & smoke — AirNow* opens the smoke map centred on the climb. See `docs/features/conditions.md`.
- **Comments and group feeds are live** (0326): a comment, reply, edit, delete or reaction another climber makes on a route, area or group post, and a post, edit, pin, delete or reaction in your groups, now appears on your screen within about a second, with no reload. `useComments` and `useGroupFeed` became `useLiveQuery` (`lib/live.js`, Supabase Realtime under the same RLS). See `docs/features/route-page.md`.
- **Moderation queues and Blocked climbers moved to the Menu**: the admin Review queues (safety reports, appeals, reported photos, guide applications, route proposals, route edits, flagged details, contributor standing, accessibility reports) left Profile's *Review* sub-tab for a *Review* tile section on the Menu, each tile with its waiting count and opening that one queue full-screen; *Blocked climbers* is always a Menu row. The speed card's unit reads on one line, *ft/hr gain · Trail / Approach*. See `docs/features/profile-and-settings.md`.
- **Camping & Bivy lists camps in the order you reach them**: the Planner's sites were grouped by use (where most parties sleep / other options / not yet sorted), so a high camp could print above the valley campground you sleep at first. They are now one list from the trailhead outward (trail distance, else height; unplaceable sites last, and the intro says so), each tagged with its use, and each says how far on and how much higher it is than the camp before. See `docs/features/route-page.md`.
- **Trip plan day cards: the pack weight sits on the same row as the other stats**: a day with gain, loss, hours, miles and pack weight wrapped the pack pill onto a line of its own on a phone (the row needed ~320px of ~294). The five pills are now tighter and share the row's width, one line down to a 360px phone.
- **Alerts remember what you read, and Unfinished business lists what waits on you**: read and archived alerts are kept on the account (`notification_state`, 0346), so a reload no longer brings back every kudos, catch and group post as new and Clear all sticks; a tap on one Home alert marks it read. *Climbs to confirm* (a partner tagged you on their log) is now an alert and a line of Unfinished business, as are a catch a partner filed that you have to confirm and an invitation to an upcoming event you have not said you are going to; the all-caught-up card counts them. See [logbook-crews-home.md](docs/features/logbook-crews-home.md).
- **Finish setting up opens on the field it asks for**: tapping *Add your climbing grades*, *Write a short bio*, *Set your availability*, *Add your home zip code* or *Add a profile photo* on Home used to open the whole profile editor at the top (availability went to the profile page), with nothing saying what to do. Each now opens the editor scrolled to that one section, outlined, under a card that names the task, why it matters and what to tap.
- **Base of the climb: one consensus, and it moves the route's Base pin** (0363): the on-site check-ins are clustered by the database (`route_base_spots`: the best-supported check-in seeds a 25 m spot, its centre is the members' median), so the map and the card read the same answer. A spot is *confirmed* when at least 3 climbers stood in it and they are most of those who checked in; a confirmed spot becomes the route's own Base pin (placed, or moved when within 200 m of the pin on file; further than that it is held for review), logged in `route_base_pin_changes` with the pin as it was. An unconfirmed spot no longer outranks the recorded pin. The unverified *Start of climb* form (Suggest a fix, and the log form's *Report start location*) and its START OF CLIMB card are gone: a map tap from home was a second, competing consensus for the same fact. The check-in now recognises all 11 spellings of a Base waypoint.
- **Crag map: the orange crag pin is easy to see**: the crag's own pin (and any derived trailhead) was a dashed ring around a 13% tint that disappeared into rock and forest; it is now an opaque dark disc with a bold dashed ring and a white halo, slightly larger. See `docs/features/maps-and-area-browser.md`.
- **Fire map: readable weather alerts, full screen, legend, satellite heat**: *Full text →* on a Red Flag Warning / Fire Weather Watch opened api.weather.gov's raw JSON (a 404 once expired); it now opens the alert's text in the app with a link to weather.gov's page for the area. A ⤢ map-only view, the area map's 📍 Me button, a legend under the map (contained = grey), and a Heat layer of satellite detections for where a fire is burning now — the dot is only where it started (`lib/FireMap.jsx`, `lib/fire.js`).
- **Profile header: disciplines get their own row**: your discipline pills (Sport, Trad, Scrambling…) sit in one full-width row under your photo and name, above the bio and photos, instead of wrapping into ragged rows beside the photo. See `docs/features/profile-and-settings.md`.
- **A topo photo stays on its own route (0364)**: a topo shows only on the route page it was posted from (`topos.route_id`, `useRouteTopos`), not on every route of the same peak or crag. See `docs/features/route-page.md`.

Save as PDF is on phones too and opens in a new tab with *← Back to ClimbMatch*. Links start from `appLink`, which takes `VITE_PUBLIC_URL` once the website has its own address. - **Topos: labelled variations, finer dots, clearer markers, photo captions (0362)**: a variation is a dashed yellow line with a tag (V, V1, V2…) on the photo and its own caption in the key (`topo_lines.variant_labels`); the editor's line dots are 7px with a 28px grab area; new *Bolt* (×) and *Crux* (⚡) markers, and *Hazard* is a warning triangle; whoever posts a topo photo can write a caption with no length limit (`topos.caption`), which everyone reads under the enlarged topo and under its thumbnail. ## Keeping this current

- A PR that changes how an area works **edits that area's doc in the same PR**, and adds one line
  to *Recent changes* above (drop the oldest past ~15).
- A new area gets a new doc and a row in the table above.
- `npm run check:feature-docs` (in the build) fails if a doc names a file or symbol that no longer
  exists, or if a doc and this index disagree. It cannot tell whether a *description* is still
  right — that half is the rule above.
