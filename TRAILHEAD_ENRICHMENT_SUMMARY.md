## Routes Enriched

### 1. Mount Goode - Northeast Buttress
**Note:** Research clarified that the main documented alpine route on Mount Goode is the **Northeast Buttress** (not "Northwest" as initially mentioned).

- **Trailhead:** Bridge Creek Trailhead (Rainy Pass)
- **Coordinates:** 48.5075°N, 120.7312°W
- **Approach Direction:** South-southeast via Pacific Crest Trail (PCT) descent, then east up North Fork Bridge Creek
- **Distance:** 22 km | **Time:** 6 hours | **Elevation Gain:** 1,829 m (6,001 ft)
- **Peak:** 48.4829°N, 120.9108°W
- **Grade:** III-IV, 5.4-5.5 climbing
- **Season:** Late July - September
- **Permits:** $10 + $6 backcountry permit from North Cascades Visitor Center (Marblemount)
- **Trip Duration:** 3-4 days recommended
- **Key Features:** Glacier travel, scrambling (3rd-4th class), rock pitches up to 5.5
- **Parking:** Large paved lot available at trailhead

### 2. Forbidden Peak - North Ridge
- **Trailhead:** Boston Basin Trailhead
- **Coordinates:** 48.4806°N, 121.0798°W
- **Approach Direction:** North-northeast via glacier traversals. From Boston Basin high camp (~5,700 ft), traverse moraine and west edge of Quien Sabe Glacier to 7,500 ft, climb to Sharkfin Col
- **Distance:** 6.0 km | **Time:** 3.5 hours | **Elevation Gain:** 1,158 m (3,800 ft)
- **Peak:** 48.5114°N, 121.0578°W
- **Grade:** III, 5.7 climbing
- **Season:** July - September
- **Access:** Register at Marblemount Ranger Station (no permit fee)
- **Required Skills:** Crevasse rescue, glacier travel
- **Hazards:** Glacier crevasses, high ridge exposure
- **Glaciers:** Quien Sabe Glacier, Boston Glacier
- **Rock Quality:** Moderate to good granite

### 3. Cutthroat Peak - North Ridge
- **Trailhead:** Pacific Crest Trailhead at Rainy Pass
- **Coordinates:** 48.5564°N, 120.6547°W
- **Approach Direction:** South-southwest via open timber basin. From PCT, hike 20 min south, leave trail at small stream, ascend through open timber to basin northwest of peak
- **Distance:** 2.0 km | **Time:** 1.5 hours | **Elevation Gain:** 762 m (2,500 ft)
- **Peak:** 48.5264°N, 120.7036°W
- **Grade:** II, 5.7 climbing
- **Season:** June - October
- **Access:** No permits; small unpaved parking at Rainy Pass trailhead
- **Notable:** Quick alpine climb from highway, uncrowded route
- **Hazards:** Steep terrain, loose rock in approach gully, exposure
- **Rock Quality:** Fair in approach, improves significantly on ridge

### 4. Mount Shuksan - White Salmon Glacier
- **Trailhead:** Lake Ann Trailhead (Austin Pass)
- **Coordinates:** 48.8502°N, 121.6861°W
- **Approach Direction:** South to Lake Ann, then west to glacier camp. Lake Ann Trail → cross creek → ridge (4,800 ft) → Lake Ann → high camp at ~6,500 ft on moraine
- **Distance:** 7.2 km | **Time:** 2.5 hours (Lake Ann to high camp) | **Elevation Gain:** 579 m (1,900 ft)
- **Peak:** 48.8611°N, 121.8194°W
- **Grade:** II-III, up to 50° ice
- **Season:** August - September (peak)
- **Access:** North Cascades National Park (no permit). Artist Point road closed after first snow (Sept-Nov), reopens by July
- **Access Road:** Mount Baker Highway (SR-542), 57 miles east from Bellingham to Mt. Baker Ski Area
- **Required Skills:** Alpine ice climbing, crevasse rescue
- **Notable:** Route options via Winnies Slide (50+ degree ice) or Fisher Chimneys
- **Hazards:** Active Hanging Glacier avalanches, bergschrunds, steep ice, crevasses
- **Descent:** Sulphide Glacier alternative descent available

### 5. Glacier Peak - Spider Glacier
- **Trailhead:** Phelps Creek Trailhead
- **Coordinates:** 48.0830°N, 120.8349°W
- **Approach Direction:** East-southeast via Phelps Creek valley to Spider Meadow, then north to Spider Gap. Follow Phelps Creek Trail (7.2 miles) through forest to Spider Meadow (~5,000 ft), ascend to Spider Gap (7,040 ft) via steep loose rock fields
- **Distance:** 13.5 km | **Time:** 4.0 hours | **Elevation Gain:** 1,067 m (3,500 ft)
- **Peak:** 48.1097°N, 121.1117°W
- **Grade:** II-III, Class 3-4 rock, various ice pitches
- **Season:** August - September (early season avalanche danger)
- **Access:** Glacier Peak Wilderness (no permit). Access via US-2 → Coles Corner → SR-207 → Chiwawa Loop Road → Chiwawa River Road (FR-62)
- **Notable:** Spider Gap connects to Lyman Lakes traverse (Glacier Peak Circumnavigation route)
- **Trail Condition:** No maintained trail to Spider Gap — requires route finding
- **Required Skills:** Route finding, alpine scrambling, glacier travel
- **Hazards:** Avalanches from hanging glaciers, loose scree fields, crevasses on glacier, poor visibility in storms, river crossings
- **Rock Quality:** Loose scree fields, poor rock quality

### 6. Mount Baker - Park Glacier
- **Trailhead:** Artist Point Trailhead
- **Coordinates:** 48.8465°N, 121.6928°W
- **Approach Direction:** Southeast via Chain Lakes Trail to Ptarmigan Ridge. Chain Lakes Trail (682) → Ptarmigan Ridge Trail (682.1) → 3 miles east → Coleman Pinnacle base → 0.5 miles to Camp Kiser (~7,000 ft)
- **Distance:** 8.0 km | **Time:** 3.5 hours (to Camp Kiser base camp) | **Elevation Gain:** 561 m (1,840 ft)
- **Peak:** 48.7769°N, 121.8142°W
- **Grade:** II, glacier travel with crevasse rescue
- **Season:** Late June - September (road closed after first snow)
- **Access:** **Recreation Pass required** at Artist Point trailhead. Glacier Public Service Center: (360) 599-2714
- **Access Road:** Mount Baker Highway (SR-542) to end at Artist Point
- **Party Limit:** Max 12 people
- **Facilities:** No campfires, no toilets, no potable water at trailhead
- **Notable:** Longest climb of northern approaches to Mount Baker. Best in early season (June-July) before glacier becomes heavily crevassed
- **Glacier Status:** 100+ km² of glaciers; iciest mountain in Cascade Range
- **Hazards:** Crevasses (major danger), heavy snow/avalanche in early season, broken glacier mid-late summer
- **Seasonal Note:** Five-mile approach can be difficult slog through heavy snow in steep chutes early season

### 7. Mount Redoubt - South/Southwest Approach
- **Trailhead:** Depot Creek Road Trailhead (British Columbia)
- **Coordinates:** 49.0217°N, 121.2542°W (Canadian side)
- **Approach Direction:** Southeast via Depot Creek and Redoubt Glacier. Approach via Depot Glacier to Redoubt Glacier; two documented routes available
- **Distance:** 17.7 km | **Time:** 5.0 hours | **Elevation Gain:** 1,524 m (5,000 ft)
- **Peak:** 48.9581°N, 121.3018°W
- **Grade:** II-III, up to 5.7 rock and 60° ice possible
- **Season:** July - September
- **Access:** **International** - Requires crossing into British Columbia, Canada. US citizens need valid passport. No permits required.
- **Access Road:** Transcanada Hwy 1 (Exit 119A) → Chilliwack → Chilliwack River Road (31.7 miles) → narrow Depot Creek FSR
- **Trailhead Condition:** Remote and narrow forest service road
- **Available Routes:**
  1. **Northeast Face** — Grade III, 60° ice + 5.5 rock (technical)
  2. **South Route** — Grade II (glacier walk + scree/snow slopes to summit)
- **Rock Quality:** Skagit Gneiss (poor quality)
- **Exposure:** High on Northeast Face route
- **Required Skills:** Alpine ice climbing, rock scrambling, glacier travel
- **Hazards:** Steep ice (60+ degrees), low 5th class rock, avalanche potential, remote location

## Data Structure

All routes updated with the following database fields:

### Fields Updated in Routes Table:

1. **`approach_logistics`** (JSONB) - Structured trailhead data
   - `trailhead` — Specific trailhead name
   - `trailheadDirection` — Compass direction + route description
   - `trailheadLat/Lng` — Trailhead coordinates
   - `peakLat/Lng` — Peak coordinates

2. **`approach`** (TEXT) - Direction and routing from trailhead

3. **`dist_km`** (NUMERIC) - Approach distance in kilometers

4. **`gain_ft`** (INTEGER) - Elevation gain in feet (converted from meters)

5. **`timing`** (JSONB) - Approach time
   - `approachTimeHrs` — Hours from trailhead to climb start

6. **`beta`** (TEXT) - Comprehensive route description with grade, hazards, features

7. **`access`** (JSONB) - Permit/fee/seasonal/hazard information
   - `notes` — Access requirements and warnings
