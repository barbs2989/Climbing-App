## Phase 2: 18 Researched Routes with Full Documentation

### Mount Rainier Routes (3)

#### 1. Fuhrer Thumb
- **Trailhead:** Paradise (46.8529°N, 121.7605°W)
- **Approach:** South-southwest via Nisqually Moraine and Wilson Gully
- **Distance:** 8 km | **Time:** 4.5 hrs | **Elevation:** 1,097 m (3,600 ft)
- **Grade:** II (50° snow/ice couloir)
- **Season:** June-September
- **Permits:** Mount Rainier National Park climbing permit ($82/person)
- **Hazards:** Ice fall from Kautz Ice Cliff, rockfall, avalanche danger, crevasses
- **Key Details:** 5-7 hours summit day from camp; requires glacier travel experience, cramponing on firm ice, two ice tools competency

#### 2. North Mowich Headwall
- **Trailhead:** Mowich Lake (46.9387°N, 121.8615°W)
- **Approach:** Northwest corner via SR 165
- **Distance:** 11 km | **Time:** 7 hrs | **Elevation:** 1,450 m (4,757 ft)
- **Grade:** IV, WI3 Moderate Snow
- **Season:** June-August (Mowich Lake Road currently CLOSED for 2026)
- **Hazards:** Steep hourglass rock/ice section (40-50°), rockfall, crevasses, avalanche debris
- **Key Details:** Bivouac at 9,600 ft recommended; two-day approach

#### 3. Edmunds Headwall
- **Status:** Route not found in documented climbing databases
- **Note:** Unable to provide trailhead documentation (route may not be officially established)

### Mount Stuart Routes (4)

#### 4. The Gendarme (Great Gendarme) - North Ridge
- **Trailhead:** Stuart Lake Trailhead (47.5278°N, 120.8205°W)
- **Approach:** Northeast via Colchuck Lake Trail → Ingalls Lake → Stuart Pass → Goat Pass → Stuart Glacier
- **Distance:** 14.5 km | **Time:** 5.5 hrs | **Elevation:** 1,463 m (4,800 ft)
- **Grade:** IV/V, 5.9 crux
- **Season:** Early summer (late June-July)
- **Permits:** Free day-use, Lottery overnight permit (May 15-Oct 31)
- **Hazards:** Glacier travel, exposure, rockfall, technical descent
- **Key Details:** 11 pitches; two-pitch crux on Gendarme itself; requires 5.7-5.9 rock and glacier travel

#### 5. South Headwall
- **Trailhead:** Esmeralda Basin Trailhead (47.4367°N, 120.9369°W)
- **Approach:** Northeast via Longs Pass Trail and Ingalls Way Trail
- **Distance:** 9.2 km | **Time:** 1.92 hrs | **Elevation:** 1,527 m (5,010 ft)
- **Grade:** 5.8 / VI- UIAA Grade II
- **Season:** June-August (peak July)
- **Permits:** Northwest Forest Pass required ($5/day or $30/annual)
- **Hazards:** Frequent rockfall, loose rock/choss, exposure
- **Key Details:** 5 pitches trad climbing; requires cams, stoppers, long slings

#### 6. King Kong - Gorillas Direct Direct
- **Trailhead:** Stuart Lake Trailhead (47.5277°N, 120.821°W)
- **Approach:** Southwest via Stuart Lake Trail; 1.25 miles to trailhead on FR 7601
- **Distance:** 7.2 km | **Time:** 4.5 hrs | **Elevation:** 507 m (1,664 ft)
- **Grade:** IV, 5.11+ alpine (5.12a on some topos)
- **Season:** Late summer through early September
- **Permits:** Northwest Forest Pass required; overnight permit through Recreation.gov
- **Hazards:** Significant runouts, loose rock, 30+ meters overhanging exposure
- **Key Details:** 8 pitches; West Face Wall; first ascent September 2016; headwall pitch 5.11d crux

#### 7. Upper North Ridge w/Great Gendarme
- **Trailhead:** Esmeralda Basin Trailhead (47.4361°N, 120.9372°W)
- **Approach:** Northeast via Lake Ingalls → Goat Pass → Stuart Glacier
- **Distance:** 6.4 km | **Time:** 4.5 hrs | **Elevation:** 914 m (3,000 ft)
- **Grade:** IV, 5.9
- **Season:** June-August
- **Permits:** Free wilderness permit (Alpine Lakes Wilderness)
- **Hazards:** Knife-edge ridge with 1,000+ feet exposure, loose rock, snow/ice conditions
- **Key Details:** 11 pitches of low/mid-5th class; two 5.9 crux pitches; 16-20 hours total trip

### Mount Shuksan Route (1)

#### 8. Southeast Ridge (SE Corner)
- **Trailhead:** Shannon Ridge Trailhead (48.7672°N, 121.6180°W)
- **Approach:** East-northeast up Shannon Ridge, traverse Sulphide Glacier to southeast ridge base
- **Distance:** 11.3 km | **Time:** 5.5 hrs | **Elevation:** 2,020 m (6,627 ft)
- **Grade:** 5.3-5.7, II-III Alpine
- **Season:** Late July-August
- **Permits:** Backcountry permit ($10/person + $6 fee)
- **Hazards:** High exposure, glacier crevasses, late-season ice, Class 4 downclimbing
- **Key Details:** 600 vertical feet over 6 pitches; often requires bivy camp

### Liberty Bell Mountain Routes (3)

#### 9. Liberty Traverse
- **Trailhead:** Blue Lake Trailhead (48.53°N, 120.668°W)
- **Approach:** South side of Highway 20, 1 mile west of Washington Pass overlook
- **Distance:** 3.2 km | **Time:** 2.5 hrs | **Elevation:** 610 m (2,000 ft)
- **Grade:** V alpine, 5.9 YDS, 26 pitches (2,000 ft)
- **Season:** Mid-June through September
- **Permits:** Northwest Forest Pass required (no additional climbing permit)
- **Hazards:** Significant rockfall in approach gully, exposed alpine terrain
- **Key Details:** Multi-route combination (Beckey Route, North Face Concord Tower, etc.); typically 1-2 day climb

#### 10. Thin Red Line
- **Trailhead:** Washington Pass East Face Trailhead (48.5245°N, 120.6581°W)
- **Approach:** Small pond on south side of Highway 20; follow cairned path eastward
- **Distance:** 1.6 km | **Time:** 1 hr | **Elevation:** 400 m (1,312 ft)
- **Grade:** 5.12 (YDS), Grade IV, 11 pitches (1,100 ft)
- **Season:** June-September (July-September strongly preferred)
- **Permits:** Northwest Forest Pass and day permit via Recreation.gov
- **Hazards:** Significant rock fall in approach gully, loose rock, exposed alpine terrain
- **Key Details:** Free ascent variation (2008); features substantial fixed gear; two-rope rappel descent

#### 11. Serpentine Crack
- **Trailhead:** Blue Lake Trail (Trail #314) (48.5197°N, 120.6741°W)
- **Approach:** West from Washington Pass area near Winthrop
- **Distance:** 2.5 km | **Time:** 2.5 hrs | **Elevation:** 280 m (920 ft)
- **Grade:** III alpine rock, 5.11 crux, 4 pitches
- **Season:** July-September (July-August optimal; June may need ice axe/crampons)
- **Permits:** Northwest Forest Pass required (no climbing permit needed)
- **Hazards:** Loose rock from freeze-thaw cycles, afternoon thunderstorms, exposure
- **Key Details:** Excellent hand crack climbing; minimal traffic; sunny arete summit

### Other Peaks (6)

#### 12. Colchuck Balanced Rock - NW Ridge
- **Trailhead:** Stuart Lake Trailhead (47.5277°N, 120.821°W)
- **Approach:** South-southeast via Icicle Creek Road to FR 7601, Colchuck Lake trail
- **Distance:** 8 km | **Time:** 3.5 hrs | **Elevation:** 800 m (2,625 ft)
- **Grade:** 5.6 alpine
- **Hazards:** Loose alpine rock, significant exposure, complex route-finding
- **Key Details:** Boulder-problem summit; 25-meter rappel descent; full car-to-car ~12 hours

#### 13. Cutthroat Creek Wall - Wright-Pond
- **Trailhead:** Cutthroat Creek Trailhead (48.556°N, 120.654°W)
- **Approach:** SR-20 to Forest Service Road 400, bushwhack 1/4 mile
- **Distance:** 2.5 km | **Time:** 1.25 hrs | **Elevation:** 400 m (1,312 ft)
- **Grade:** 5.11a/b trad, 4 pitches
- **Season:** Late June-September
- **Permits:** Northwest Forest Pass or America the Beautiful Pass (digital via recreation.gov)
- **Hazards:** Lichen, loose rock, early season snow/ice
- **Key Details:** FA October 4, 2010; FS-400 semi-paved; no maintained trail

#### 14. Glacier Peak - Disappointment Cleaver
- **Trailhead:** North Fork Sauk River Trailhead (48.0595°N, 121.2890°W)
- **Approach:** North Fork Sauk River Trail to Mackinaw Shelter, then White Pass to Disappointment Cleaver
- **Distance:** 14.5 km | **Time:** 8 hrs | **Elevation:** 1,372 m (4,501 ft)
- **Grade:** Grade I Glacier / 3rd-4th Class Rock
- **Season:** May-October (July recommended)
- **Permits:** Northwest Forest Pass ($5/day); Free wilderness permit
- **Hazards:** Loose rock, crevasses, avalanche terrain, high winds, rockfall
- **Key Details:** 2-4 day recommended; rope, crampons, ice axe essential; Darrington Ranger District (360) 436-1155

#### 15. Mount Goode - Northeast Face
- **Trailhead:** Rainy Pass PCT Trailhead (48.5181°N, 120.7331°W)
- **Approach:** South on Pacific Crest Trail 9.6 miles to North Fork Bridge Creek Trail, then north with bushwhacking
- **Distance:** 25.7 km | **Time:** 8.5 hrs | **Elevation:** 1,463 m (4,801 ft)
- **Grade:** IV, 5.5 alpine rock (6-8 pitches)
- **Season:** Mid-July (snowmelt conditions)
- **Permits:** Backcountry permit ($10/person + $6 fee) through Recreation.gov
- **Hazards:** Glacial crevasses/seracs, loose rock, rockfall, sun exposure
- **Key Details:** Requires glacier travel with crampons; 2.2 miles, 3,300-3,800 ft elevation gain; ~10 hours

#### 16. Liberty Cap - Liberty Ridge
- **Trailhead:** White River Campground (46.9024°N, 121.6438°W)
- **Approach:** Glacier Basin Trail → St. Elmo Pass → Winthrop Glacier → Curtis Ridge → Carbon Glacier
- **Distance:** 9.6 km | **Time:** 6.67 hrs | **Elevation:** 2,959 m (9,707 ft)
- **Grade:** IV alpine ice, 40-60° steep snow/ice, 5.7 pitches
- **Season:** May 27 - June 8 (best); winter conditions possible into July
- **Permits:** $82 annual climbing fee; wilderness permit $10/person/night
- **Hazards:** Crevasses, heavy snow/avalanche early season, broken glacier mid-late summer
- **Key Details:** Requires crevasse rescue training, multi-pitch ice climbing, established belay skills

#### 17. Mount Baker - Cockscomb Ridge
- **Trailhead:** Artist Point (48.8465°N, 121.6927°W)
- **Approach:** Ptarmigan Ridge Trail to Camp Kiser, cross Coleman and Roosevelt Glaciers
- **Distance:** 10.5 km | **Time:** 7 hrs | **Elevation:** 2,195 m (7,201 ft)
- **Grade:** 4th class YDS, AI2-3 ice, M1b mixed, PG-13
- **Season:** Spring or early summer (late season loose rock creates high hazard)
- **Permits:** Northwest Forest Pass ($5 day use or $30 annual); Free self-issue wilderness permit
- **Hazards:** Extremely loose/unstable rock, bergschrunds, extensive crevassing, icefall terrain
- **Key Details:** 3,000 ft vertical gain over 5 pitches; FA July 4, 1961; descent via Coleman-Deming Route

#### 18. Johannesburg Mountain - Northeast Ridge (1963 Route)
- **Trailhead:** Cascade Pass Trailhead (48.4683°N, 121.06°W)
- **Approach:** East/Northeast from Cascade Pass, cross Cascade River via C-J couloir
- **Distance:** 4.83 km | **Time:** 3 hrs | **Elevation:** 1,391 m (4,564 ft)
- **Grade:** 5.7+, Grade IV, 12 pitches (4,000 ft)
- **Season:** July-September
- **Permits:** Wilderness permit required year-round (Recreation.gov: 60% advance, 40% first-come-first-serve)
- **Hazards:** Objective danger from falling rock/ice, steep snow, exposed scrambling, avalanche potential
- **Key Details:** FA 1963; ascend leftmost rib to summit; no bush climbing
