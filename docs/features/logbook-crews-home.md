# Logbook, Crews and Home — `logbook`, `crew`, `today` tabs

Three top-level `tab` values in `ClimbMatch.jsx` (`tab==="today"`, `tab==="crew"`, `tab==="logbook"`).
`logbook` is split by `logbookTab` (`useState("objectives")`: `objectives`, `lists`, `completed`, `challenges`);
`crew` by `crewView` (`crews`, `friends`, `requests`; Friends and Requests open from the Menu tiles).

## 1. What it does today
- **Crew share link** (0381, 2026-10-28): in the Crew sheet's *Crew settings* (it was a *⋯ More* sheet until 2026-11-18), a crew shows *Copy share link* (any member; `appLink("crew="+shareToken)`) and, for the organiser only, a *Share link* On/Off switch (`crews.share_on`, through `updateCrewRow`). The link stops working on its own after the crew’s climb date. See `docs/features/partners.md` for what the link opens.
- **Logbook > Objectives / Saved**: `ListsManager` (`lib/ListsManager.jsx`) holds the objectives (wishlist) and saved lists. The Objectives filter My Objectives draws no card around its objective cards (one surface, 2026-11-23): its count, Select, Add climbs, Share and Save as PDF sit on the page. The header carries no explainer paragraph under it (owner note, 2026-11-25): the rows' own controls say what they do.
- **A guest's lists are kept on the device** (owner, 2026-11-24): signed out, My Objectives and every list the guest keeps are written to localStorage (`cm_guest_lists`, `writeGuestLists` in `lib/guestLists.js`) and read back when the app opens (`readGuestLists`, in `userLists`' initial state). Only lists with no `_dbId` are written, only while nobody is signed in and the session check has finished, and never in a visit where someone WAS signed in (`everSignedInRef`): signing out leaves the account's objectives in state, and writing them would hand them to the phone's next user. Signing in replaces them with the account's, as before. Until then "Saved on this device" and "Added to … on this device" were claims of React state only, gone on reload.
  chips, in order: **Alpine, Mountaineering, Sport, Trad, Aid, Ice & Mixed, Boulder, Scrambling** — Alpine leads, and alpine,
  mountaineering and aid are each their own chip rather than folded into Alpine or Trad (#2409, then this split). A route with a 5.x grade is never on the Mountaineering chip, whatever its discipline list says (`rDiscs`). Saved no longer repeats a "Pinned states" card;
  Home already shows it (#2408).
  Under the chips sit two selects: **Sort** (`oSort`, kept on the device as `cm.objSort`; the list is `OBJ_SORTS`: Newest first — the default — Target day, Name A–Z, Grade; a logged climb sorts below the ones still to do in every order) and **Location** (only when the climbs span more than one state). They are always on screen. There is no Status filter and no Sections select (both removed, owner 2026-11-19: every climb here is an objective, the discipline chips pick the discipline and Location picks the state), so My Objectives and every custom list are one flat list. Each climb is its own inset card: a numbered badge and the name, grade and where it is (a *Logged* pill only on a logged climb; there is no *To do* pill, since every objective is to do), then the actions under a hairline: *Find partners* solid, *Log* and *Target day* quiet. All three are drawn from one geometry, `ACT_BOX` in `lib/ListsManager.jsx` (a fixed 40px border-box height), so the *Target day* box, a `<span>` over an invisible date input, is exactly as tall as the two buttons on every engine; they grow to share the row, and the box's words (*Target day*, or the day once set) are never cut off. Only a card too narrow for all three at full width (a 320px phone) drops the box to a full-width line of its own (owner note 2026-11-25). The card itself is not a control: the name-and-details block opens the climb, and the action buttons are its siblings, so no button sits inside another (axe `nested-interactive`). There is no ✕ on a card (removed, owner 2026-11-19): **Select** removes one climb or several, with a confirm. My Objectives has no header band; its count (*4 climbs to go*) sits on the Select row. Only while selecting is the whole card a checkbox, and then it holds no other control.

See them under Objectives*) switches to that sub-tab. Saved areas is therefore the top box.
- **Logbook > Saved: add an area or a peak from its box** (owner 2026-11-23): each of the two boxes ends in *+ Add an area* / *+ Add a peak* (`src/components/SavedPlaceAdd.tsx`). It opens a search over the whole catalog (`useCatalogAreaSearch`, split on `area_type`: peaks in the peaks box, everything else but the world in the areas box); a tap on a hit saves it through `toggleBookmark`, the same call as the area page's Save button, and a hit already saved reads *✓ Saved*. The box stays open until *Done* so several can be added; a failed search says so rather than reading as no match. Signed out with the demo content showing, the demo's own places answer too.

Each row names the climb, its grade and place, how long ago it was saved and whether its map came with it; tapping it opens the route page from the device copy, *Update* saves it again, and a swipe removes it (a button beside the row on the website). Routes only: downloaded states stay on Home's pinned card and Manage areas, which is why the first Offline chip (2026-10-24, states + maps) was removed in #3406. - **Logbook > Saved > Peaks / Areas**: a LIST, one row per saved place — a pin (a mountain for a peak), the name, its type and parent (*Crag · in Leavenworth*, from `useAreaCardsByIds`' `type` and `parentName`), and a › that opens it; the ✕ at the row's end unsaves through `toggleBookmark`. They used to be wrapping pills (owner note 2026-10-24), then bordered boxes one under another; since 2026-10-29 each section is ONE card whose rows are divided by a hairline (owner note: "more of a list rather than just separate boxes").
- **New custom list** (`ListsManager`'s *+ Create custom list*, `clStep`): two steps in one sheet, as AllTrails and Google Maps do it. Step 1 asks only a **Name** and an optional **Description** (the 500-character counter shows past 400), with *Next: add climbs* and *Create without climbs*; step 2 is the climb picker (`DbClimbPicker`), titled *Add climbs to <name>* with *← Name & description*, and *Create list (N)* saves. - **A logged climb and a trip report are ONE `climb_logs` row** (reviewed 2026-10-21). The difference is how much the climber filled in and which way they answered *DID YOU CLIMB IT?*:
  - *Quick log* = a climb with a style, a date and maybe stars. *Full log* (`LogAscent`) = the same row with the seven sections filled in; its write-up box (`notes`) is what makes it read as a trip report, and `TripReport` is how any row opens.
  - *Conditions only* (`tick_type` `Conditions`, App's `condReports` rather than `logs`) = a report from someone who may not have climbed: road, snow, crowds. It feeds conditions and is never counted as a climb. It does count toward your *Log conditions on 5 routes* challenge (`Challenges` takes `condReports`).
  - Every row, climb or conditions only, feeds the route page: the Reports tab, the conditions consensus (`buildConsensus` over `activity`; a report kept to friends or to yourself still counts, anonymously, through `route_private_conditions`), the crowd readout and the alpine Conditions card's freshness. It also feeds Home's recent condition reports and offline state downloads (`state_trip_reports`). Saving one of either kind raises the *still missing …* alert that opens the contribute form (`missingFacts`), and records a sun vote if one was cast.
  - Only a climb (not Conditions) feeds the climber's side: Completed, Challenges, the profile résumé, sends and points on Ranks (`leaderboard()`; Attempt, Turned around, Fell, Hung and Project are logged but are not sends), Unfinished business (`RetryReminder`), *Logged the same day* pairings (`route_logged_with`, Conditions excluded since 0336), a crew's past state and recap, partner tags and the vouch prompt, a first-ascent credit and the send celebration. A public report's stars are the route rating's fallback vote (`route_star_summary`), and Ranks › *Top climbs* counts public reports per route.
  - Both earn contribution points since 0338 (owner: "reward all"): a logged climb or attempt 1, once per route; a trip report (a write-up, conditions tags, beta, or a Conditions-only report) 2, once per route per month. Only a row shared with Everyone counts, because the boards are public and name the climber per area; this is Strava's rule for public leaderboards. A private log still counts toward your own Challenges.
- **Select mode** (`ListsManager`'s *Select*, `selL`/`selIds`, owner 2026-10-24): each open list (My Objectives and every custom list) has a *Select* button. Rows turn into checkboxes (`role="checkbox"`), the ✕, crew, Log and target-day controls hide, and a bar shows *N selected · Select all/Clear · Done* over *Share N climbs* and *Remove N climbs*. Share hands App's list share sheet a part of the list (`_part`, named *N climbs from <list>*), which always goes as each climb with its own `?route=` link, since the list's own link would open all of it. Remove asks *Remove N climbs from <list>?* first. Both act only on ticked climbs the filters still show. This is the phone's own multi-select (Photos, Gmail, Google Maps' saved lists); AllTrails and Mountain Project remove one item at a time.
- **One writer for a list's climbs** (`setListIds` in `ListsManager`): every edit (the row ✕ after its confirm, Remove selected, *Add climbs*) goes through it. A custom list writes `user_lists` (`onUpdateList`); **My Objectives writes the `objectives` table** through App's `onObjectivesChanged` (`saveObjective`/`removeObjective` on the difference; a failure toasts and re-reads). Before 2026-10-24 My Objectives had no writer here, so a removed objective came back on the next load.
- **Loading is not empty** (`routesLoading` / `routesFailed` on `ListsManager`, from `useRoutesByIds`): a list whose saved ids have not all resolved to climbs yet says *Loading N climbs…*, or *Couldn't load N of the climbs on this list…* if the read failed, never *Nothing here yet*. `useRoutesByIds` keeps its previous rows (`placeholderData`) while a changed id set refetches, so adding or removing one climb does not blank the others. Home's *My objectives* tile counts saved ids (`wishlist`), and Recent condition reports says *Checking your objectives…* while they load.
- **The grade line names the discipline in words** (owner note, "a little weird icon" beside the grade): each My Objectives and Completed row reads *Trad · 5.8* (`DISC_LABELS` + `gradeLabel`), with no glyph; the place (peak, then range or area) is its own pin line under it.

A custom list stores its climbs as one array with no per-climb date, so its rows show none and that sort falls back to the order they were added.
- **Sharing a list** (`ListsManager`'s *Share…*, `onShareList` → App's list share sheet): **Send to a friend in ClimbMatch** first (a chat from you in their Messages), then `ShareVia` (Text, Email, Copy link, More…), then Save as PDF (`printSheet` + `listSheetHtml`, a new tab with *← Back to ClimbMatch*). - **Sharing a trip report** (`TripReport`'s 🔗, 2026-10-22): the sheet offers Share everything (the native share sheet, with photos where the device allows), Copy link, Copy text, Email, Facebook, Instagram (copies the caption) and Download (.txt). ## 2. How it works
1. `App` reads server state through React Query hooks in `lib/db.js`: `useMyObjectives`, `useMyLogs` (your own logs; `useUserLogs` and `useClimberReports` read another climber's), `useMyCrews`, `useMyItineraries`,
   `useMyCrewInvites`, `useMyObjectiveSavedAt`. Writes are `saveObjective`/`removeObjective`, `createClimbLog`/`updateClimbLog`/`deleteClimbLog`,
   `createCrew`/`updateCrewRow`/`deleteCrewRow`, `addCrewMember`/`updateCrewMemberStatus`/`removeCrewMember`, `ackCrewDay`/`unackCrewDay`.
2. **Ready state**: `datesAgreed(c)` / `agreedDate(c)` in `ClimbMatchCore.jsx`: a crew is Ready only when every confirmed member (ME included)
   has acked the same proposed day (`dayAcks`, persisted in `crew_day_acks`). `isArchivedCrew(c,logs)` in the same file decides "past".
3. **Crew gear**: `CrewGearList` calls `addCrewGearItem`, `assignCrewGear`, `removeCrewGearItem` (RPCs `add_crew_gear_item`, `assign_crew_gear`, `remove_crew_gear_item`);
   `useCrewGearSync` and `pingCrewGear` refresh other members. #2395 serialized gear writes and migration 0291 made `assign_crew_gear` accept only confirmed, listed members.
   Since 0328 an item can carry how many the crew wants (`setCrewGearWanted`, RPC `set_crew_gear_wanted`, `crew_members.gear_wanted`) and each bringer how many they bring
   (`setCrewGearCount`, RPC `set_crew_gear_count`, `crew_members.gear_counts`); several climbers can bring one item and the counts add up, so "Quickdraws · 12 wanted" is
   covered only at 12. The list is only what the crew typed: nothing is drawn from the route or the members' profiles, and the card suggests no gear.
4. **Messaging**: `sendCrewMessage`/`fetchOlderCrewMessages`, `sendDirectMessage`/`fetchMyDirectMessages`, read markers via `markCrewRead`/`fetchCrewLastReads`/`markDmThreadRead`.
5. **Pinned states** follow the account (0187; Home's card says so only to a guest, in one line — the signed-in explainer sentence was removed 2026-11-18): `pinnedStates` state in `App`, saved by `savePinnedStates` (`profiles.pinned_states`). Offline downloads stay on the device
   (`downloadedStates`, `dlLoaded`, `lib/offline.js`: `downloadStateOffline`, `removeStateOffline`, `packRouteOffline`).
   Home's pinned card (`TodayTab`) lists the pins A to Z, one row each: the name on the left, and on the right the
   device copy's age from `dlTimes` ("Last updated 3h ago") with an Update button (`updateState`), or Download for a pin
   not on this device yet. Until this device's downloads are read (`dlLoaded`) a row shows neither, so a saved
   state never flashes "Not on this device · Download".
   **Update all** (owner note 2026-11-25): a small button beside the card's heading, and beside the *N on this
   device* line on Manage areas, runs `updateAllStates` in `App`: every state on this device, then every pinned state
   not on it yet, one at a time, reading *Updating 2 of 5…*, with one toast at the end naming any that failed.
   **One queue** (`runDl`, `dlChainRef`): a download, an Update, Update all, a removal and the quiet once-a-session
   refresh run one after another, never two on the same state at once.
   **A refresh never loses the saved copy** (2026-11-25): `downloadStateFromSnapshot` and `downloadStateLive` write
   the new rows over a COMPLETE copy and prune what the catalog dropped only at the end (`idbPruneState`,
   `completeMeta`), keeping the state marked complete throughout; a refresh that fails or is cut off leaves the old
   copy readable. They used to mark it incomplete and delete it first, so an app closed mid-refresh came back with
   the state gone and Home offering Download for it. Proven by `scripts/oneoff/probe-state-refresh-keeps-copy.mjs`.
   A download lives in IndexedDB, asks the browser to keep it (`navigator.storage.persist()`), and is read back by
   `offlineDownloads()` on every launch, so it survives closing the app; a pin made on another device downloads here.
   It comes from the nightly state file (`scripts/build-state-snapshots.mjs`) when that file is under 3 days old, else
   live from the database (`downloadStateLive`), which reads routes BY AREA id (`AREA_BATCH`): the old path-join read
   timed out on every state. A state the nightly build cannot read is left out of that night's file, not the whole set.
   The build reads trip reports through `state_trip_reports` with the ANON key, so that function must read only
   columns anon holds: 0501 took `climb_logs.partners` from anon and the function's `select l.*` failed every state
   for three nights (2026-11-23 to 25), until 0540 named its columns.
   A downloaded state answers the Climbs tab from the device first (`deviceFirst` in `lib/db.js`), except the Route finder's
   list and count over more than 2,000 climbs (`deviceFirstScan`, `DEVICE_SCAN_MAX_ROUTES`): the device has no index to filter
   or sort on, so a whole pinned state scanned all its climbs and the Washington page's Suggested climbs waited seconds for it.
   Those ask the database first and keep the device copy as the no-signal fallback. A smaller subtree reads only its own
   areas' climbs (`localSubtreeRouteCount`; `matchingRoutes` by the `area_id` index), not the whole state's.
   **Manage areas** (`manageAreasOpen`, from Home's pinned card) lists every state or province of the picked country with its
   catalog count from `useStates()`. With the database on, a count is the database's or nothing: while that read is in flight a
   row says *Loading the catalog…* (`statesLoading`), and a failed read says so (`statesUnavailable`); neither falls back to
   the four-state seed tree, which used to grey out 46 states as "No climbs in the catalog" until the read landed.
   The country choice (United States · Canada) is always shown: `KNOWN_COUNTRIES` and `CA_PROVINCES` in `lib/countries.js`
   list every country and province before the read lands and when it fails, so Canada is offered at once, offline included;
   it used to appear only once `useStates()` returned, so the screen read as US-only for the first 8-28s.
5a. **Saved areas and peaks** follow the account (0322): one `bookmarks` list in `App` feeds the area page's Save button, Logbook › Saved's ✕, the Home and Menu tiles, so they always agree.
   `toggleBookmark` writes `saveAreaRow`/`removeAreaRow` (`saved_areas`, owner-only) and toasts only after the write; a failure restores the list. `useMySavedAreas` is copied into `bookmarks`;
   `lib/offline.js` (`saveAreaIds`, `savedAreaIds`, `savedAreasSynced`) is the offline mirror, and a device's pre-0322 saves are uploaded the first time the account is read there.
6. **Logs**: a log can name partners (`saveLogPartnerNames`, `useMyLogPartnerNames`); tagged climbers answer with `respondToLogTag` (RPC `respond_to_log_tag`, `useLogTagsForMe`).
7. **Vouches**: `giveVouch`, `updateVouch`, `revokeVouch`; read with `useClimberVouches`, `useUserVouches`.
