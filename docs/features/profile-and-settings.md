# Profile, résumé and settings — the `me` tab and the Settings sheet

`tab==="me"` in `ClimbMatch.jsx`. It is the bottom bar's **right-most tab**, and it also opens from the Menu
(`menuOpen`, the avatar button top left), whose "You" card leads here. Settings is a separate full-screen dialog
(`settingsOpen` / `setSettingsOpen`, `aria-label="Settings"`) also opened from the Menu. A climber's own or another's
profile can also open as `FullProfile` and `Resume` overlays from anywhere (partner rows, crews, leaderboards).

## 1. What it does today
- **Stat line under the name** (competitor review batch A, item 8, owner 2026-11-04): *N climbs · N vouches* on your own header, the same two figures the AT A GLANCE card prints below (your logs, `communityVouches`), so the two never disagree; a public profile (`FullProfile`) prints *N climbs logged · N vouches* for a real climber from the Logged Climbs count and `communityVouches`. A zero is left out; nothing at all until there is a number.
- **Vouch stars under the name** (owner 2026-11-23): once at least one vouch rated, `FullProfile`'s header shows `Stars` with `outOf={5}` (filled to the average, the rest dimmed), the average to one decimal, and *N people vouched* — every vouch counts, and *· N rated* is added when fewer rated than vouched, since the average reads only the rated ones (`vouchAvg`). It is a button that opens the Vouches tab. While that row shows, the stat line above drops its *N vouches* so the count is printed once.
- **Reliability under the name** (0519, owner 2026-11-23): `FullProfile` and your own Profile tab (`MeTab`) print what crewmates reported after crew trips, as counts (`reliabilityText`): *Showed up for 9 of 10 crew trips · 1 cancelled last minute*. Read with `useReliabilityOf` (signed-in only); nothing until a trip is on record and nothing while the read is out, so a failed read never reads as a clean record. Never a score, badge or ring: the trust score was removed on 2026-10-21. How it is recorded: `docs/features/logbook-crews-home.md` (*How did it go?*).
- **Profile (`me`)**: your header (verification, photo strip, résumé and public-profile buttons) stays on top; *Edit profile* · *Settings* are a full-width row of two under your name and bio, not boxes in the card's top-right corner — your photo and name, then your disciplines as one full-width row of pills from the card's left edge (not squeezed into the column beside the photo, where six wrapped into three ragged rows), then your bio and the photo strip; under it, tabs (`meTab`, read as `meTabOn`): **Profile** (At a glance, availability, speed, certifications, vouches you’ve received (`myVouchesShown`, the two newest in full and *See all*), vouches you’ve given, Edit profile; At a glance, Availability and Certifications & Skills each carry a ✎ *Edit* (`MeEdit`) that opens the editor scrolled to that part, `ep-grades` / `ep-availability` / `ep-certs`) · **Activity** (Year in Climbing banner, Your Logbook, sends by grade, reports you’ve filed). Home’s availability gap opens Profile. Walks reach a tab with `?mt=` (overlay scaffold). The speed card prints its number over ONE line, *ft/hr gain · Trail / Approach*.
- **Résumé header** (`src/components/Resume.tsx`): name, headline and discipline pills, with NO email-verified chip (owner 2026-11-23: verification belongs on the profile, not the résumé). Its stat row is four cells: Sport · Trad · Boulder · Climbs (the *Unique* count was removed the same day).
- **No Review section on the Menu** (owner 2026-11-25: "Is the review section on menu only for me as the owner of the app? If so, remove since we created the admin page"). It was admins-only (`isAdmin`) and, by its last day, held four tiles: Route proposals, Area proposals, Wrong place claims and Feedback & ratings. All four are sections of the owner's Review desk (`review.html`, `review/ReviewDesk.jsx`): proposals at `#proposals`, *Wrong place claims* at `#placements`, *Feedback & ratings* at `#feedback`. The full-screen sheet those tiles opened (and its `reviewQueue` state) is deleted, and the Menu button no longer carries a waiting count or says one in its name: that count was the Review section's. History: a *Review* sub-tab on Profile until 2026-10-23, then Menu tiles; Safety reports, Appeals and Reported photos removed 2026-10-24 (read in the Supabase dashboard); Route edits and Flagged details to the desk 2026-11-14; Accessibility reports and Trails & GPS tracks 2026-11-23; Contributor standing 2026-11-24.

Tile groups: *Plan & log* (Log a climb, Your ascents, Add a climb, Float plans, Your conditions; Float plans opens the Crews view and keeps polling for the lazily drawn Float plans box before scrolling to it, since a one-shot timer fired before the chunk mounted and left the page at the top; `scrollToWhenMounted` then watches for ~3 s and scrolls again if the tab-change reset pushed the section back out of view (one tap in three still landed at the top before that), stopping at the climber's first scroll, tap or key; the Downloads tile, which only scrolled Home to the pinned-states card, came out 2026-11-05, and Offline library and Hire a guide followed 2026-11-11 -- downloads live in Logbook › Saved › Offline; the guide feature itself was removed 2026-11-14) · *Saved & lists* (Saved areas opens Logbook › Saved with `savedKind` set to `areas`; Everything saved; Objectives & your lists; Challenges; Climbing lists sets `challengeListsAt`, which `Challenges` reads as `listsAt` to open its curated-lists dialog) · *Yours* (Friends, Crews · N, Groups, Requests (its waiting count, friend + crew + group requests + crew invites, is a red badge in the tile's top-right corner like the header icons' badges, not " · N" on the label), Listed areas, contributions, Year in Climbing, share/invite, guide).

  - **Home city is PICKED from a town catalog (0544), and is still a LABEL, never geocoded.** `src/components/HomeTownField.tsx`
    lists matching towns as you type (`useHomeTowns` → the `places_search` RPC over `places`: ~199k rows — every US
    Census place (2025 Gazetteer, incorporated + CDP), the USGS GNIS populated places the Census does not draw (Mazama
    WA, a hamlet, is in no Census file) and Canada's census subdivisions and settlements from Wikidata — all public
    domain or CC0, so no on-screen credit). A pick fills the city AND the state dropdown and records WHICH town in
    `profiles.home_place_id` (owner-only, read through `my_profile`), so "Mt Vernon" and "Mount Vernon" are one home and
    two Springfields are two; the row shows the county to tell namesakes apart. Ranking: names that START with the
    query, the exact name first, your chosen state next, then the bigger town (`pop`, a ranking key never shown); a
    3+-letter query that leaves room also matches every word anywhere ("vernon"). Typing over a pick, or choosing a
    different state, clears the id. A town the catalog lacks still saves AS TYPED, and the line under the box says
    which happened; a failed search says so, never "no such town". The label shows on your profile and partner rows,

- **No trust score** (removed 2026-10-21, owner decision): no screen computes, shows, filters, sorts or gates on one. What a climber judges a partner by is the ✓ (verification) and the vouches they received. 0335 dropped `compute_trust_score` and `trust_scores_view` from the database too.
- **Verification** (0306): ✓ means you proved you read your inbox. Sign-up's autoconfirm no longer counts. `VerifyEmailPanel` on Profile emails a one-time code (`sendEmailCode`), you type it (`confirmEmailCode`) or tap the link in the same email, and `verifyMyEmail` writes the record; the server's `verify_my_email()` accepts only a session whose `amr` shows a code, link or reset link from the last 30 minutes. Your own ✓ comes from the record (`useVerificationRecords`), never the session; a failed read decides nothing and hides the "Verify" prompts (`verificationUnavailable`). Until a mail provider (SMTP) is connected, Supabase's built-in mailer only delivers to the project's team, and the panel says so. **There is no ID verification** (0339, owner decision 2026-10-21): no screen asks for an identity document, `verification_records` no longer accepts the kind `id`, and the unused client writer that could file one is gone. Email is the only verification a climber does.
- **Sign out** (Menu's *Sign out*, Settings' *Sign out* — one word for it since round 5): `signOut` ends the session on the server, drops every cached per-climber query, and goes back to signed-out. If the logout request cannot reach the server (offline, outage), it still signs THIS device out — supabase-js would otherwise keep the session while the app said "Signed out" — and the server-side refresh token simply expires.

## 2. How it works
1. **Real vs seed.** `ME` (`ClimbMatchCore.jsx`) is the seed global. `App` re-copies hook state onto it each render (legacy hack; do not extend).
   When signed in, `getProfile(id)` (`lib/auth.js`, `profiles` select `*`; `PGRST116` zero-rows returns `null`, any other error throws) hydrates `profile` (`useState` + `setProfile`)
   and the rest of the app reads it through `meLive`. The sign-in reset zeroes `ME`, so new code spreads `meLive` rather than `ME`.
2. **Cold load**: until `getProfile()` lands `ME.name` is the placeholder "Climber"; the Today greeting uses the name this device remembered for the account; with none remembered it is drawn invisible (`greetPending`, the line keeps its height) until the profile read settles, and only then falls back to the session's sign-up name. Never the placeholder, and never the sign-up name ahead of the profile: a climber who changed "Nathan" to "Nate" saw one replace the other on every new device. The greeting reads `profile.name` only once `pinsFor===uid` (set in the same batch as the profile hydrate, cleared on every account change): `profile` starts as the seed climber, and the reset that empties it is an effect that runs after the frame the session arrives on, so that frame said "Good morning, Nathan".
   App paints a blank shell until `useSession` first answers (`session===undefined`) even with demo autologin on, as production is, or the demo climber's home flashes for a frame before the account's.
   With a backend, a frame with NO account greets with no name at all, never `ME.name`: that frame is a guest, or a signed-in climber whose expired token is still refreshing (supabase-js answers `getSession()` with null when the refresh fails, and a tab with `cm_guest` set then paints guest Home), and `ME` there is the seed climber.
3. **Edit**: opening the editor seeds `editDraft`; `saveEdit` normalises it (`FALLBACK_AV` avatar placeholder becomes `""`, never stored; `setMyWeek(d.availWeek)`),
   then calls `saveProfile(id, fields)` (`lib/auth.js`), a PATCH of whatever object it is handed that invalidates every cached profile reader (`authProfileReaders`).
   Because it PATCHes a partial object blindly, a failed profile read must not open the editor (`check:profile-edit-gate`) and the photo strip uses
   its own one-column write (`saveProfilePhotos`). The **avatar** is not drafted either: "Change photo" uploads and then writes `profiles.avatar`
   at once (`saveProfileAvatar`, one column), and "Remove photo" clears it at once (`removeProfileAvatar`); both tell App through `onAvatarChange`.
   It used to wait for Save, so Cancel dropped an uploaded photo, and Save is disabled until the climber has a username, which no signup sets
   (`handle_new_user` stores only `id, name`). The empty-username reason now shows without touching the field.
   Since 2026-10-28 a signed-in climber with no username opens the editor with one already suggested (`suggestUsername`: the name's
   letters plus four digits, editable), so that first Save is never dead; and a username another climber holds (`23505` on the unique
   column) no longer sinks the whole edit: `saveEdit` re-sends the rest without it and says the handle is taken.
   `scripts/oneoff/audit-profile-photos-live.mjs` re-reads every stored avatar and strip photo over plain HTTP, as a stranger's `<img>` does.
   Walked end to end by `scripts/oneoff/probe-profile-photos-stick.mjs` (fixture accounts, headless Chrome).
4. **Other climbers**: `useFullProfile(userId)` and `fetchProfilesByIds` (`lib/db.js`) feed `FullProfile` / `Resume`, and `useClimberResume(userId)` their résumé's hand-written parts (0342); `profiles` is public read, blocked users cannot read it (0095).
5. **Résumé groups**: `Resume` maps `GROUPS` (the six labels above) over the climber's ticks; each tick resolves to a group via its discipline (`catOf`).
   Seed ticks hand-tagged `kind:"Crag"`/`"Other"` were re-tagged Trad/Scrambling in #2415, otherwise they matched no group and vanished.
6. **Units / date**: `units` and `dateFmt` are `App` state; each render calls `__set_UNITS(units)` and `__set_DLOCALE(dateFmtToLocale(dateFmt))`
   so module-level `UNITS` / `DLOCALE` (read by formatters) follow. Stored on-device by `loadUnits`/`saveUnits` (`lib/units-pref.js`, key `climbmatch-units`)
   and `loadDateFmt`/`saveDateFmt` (`lib/date-pref.js`); `currentDateLocale()` reads it outside React.
   *Also show grades in* (French · UIAA · Ewbank · Font, all off by default) is `lib/grade-scales-pref.js` (`useGradeScales`/`setGradeScale`,
   key `climbmatch-grade-scales`), read by the route page's Composite Grade line, not App state.
7. **Notification and browse switches** are flag sets: `loadNotifPrefs`/`saveNotifPrefs` (`lib/notif-pref.js`, `climbmatch-notif-prefs`) — the notification switches ALSO follow a signed-in account since 0469 (`notification_state.prefs`, read and saved with the alerts read-state by `useMyNotificationState`/`saveMyNotificationState`; a switch the account saved wins over the device, and the device's are written up on first load),
   `visibleWhileBrowsingPref`/`saveVisibleWhileBrowsing` (`lib/browse-visibility-pref.js`), built on `lib/prefs.js` (`defineFlagSet`). Onboarding shown-once: `lib/onboard-pref.js`.
8. **Server counts**: gone. `my_trust_counts` (0203) and its reader `useMyTrustCounts` were removed with belay catches (0417, 2026-11-12). A future per-user count RPC keys its query on the user only, never on another query's `dataUpdatedAt` — that fired the RPC on every logs refetch.
