   - Status: CRITICAL — highest-traffic peak, rescue-critical data missing
   
3. **North Cascades Passes** (15-18 routes)
   - Hazards: Icefall, cornices, crevasse entry, Ptarmigan Traverse
   - Status: HIGH — popular trailhead with technical mountain hazards
   
4. **Leavenworth Granite** (18-22 routes)
   - Hazards: Loose rock endemic, lichen, water seepage, bolt quality
   - Status: HIGH — busiest WA trad destination, loose-rock profile underdocumented
   
5. **Snoqualmie Crags** (12-16 routes)
   - Hazards: Loose rock, wet seepage, flash flood (Denny Creek), traffic
   - Status: HIGH — Seattle-area popular climbing
   
6. **Index Sandstone** (10-14 routes)
   - Hazards: Fragile rock, bolt corrosion, poor protection
   - Status: MEDIUM-HIGH — unique geology hazards
   
7. **Enchantments & Secondary Peaks** (30-35 routes)
   - Hazards: Scramble exposure, loose rock, routefinding, weather
   - Status: MEDIUM-HIGH — popular destination, underestimated hazards
   
8. **Winter/Seasonal Routes** (25-30 routes)
   - Hazards: Avalanche, wind slab, crevasse dynamics (distinct from summer)
   - Status: MEDIUM — NWAC data exists but not integrated

Before launching agents, validate these routes exist in live Supabase:

```sql
-- Count routes by area that should have hazards documented
SELECT area_name, COUNT(*) as route_count, 
       SUM(CASE WHEN hazard_tags IS NULL THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) as missing_hazards
FROM routes
WHERE area_name IN (
  'Glacier Peak', 'Mount Rainier', 'Mount Baker', 'Mount Adams',
  'Cascade Pass', 'Leavenworth Granite', 'Snoqualmie Falls', 'Index',
  'Enchantments', 'Goat Rocks', 'Mount Shuksan'
)
GROUP BY area_name
ORDER BY missing_hazards DESC;
```
