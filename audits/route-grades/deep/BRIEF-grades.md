# Deep grade research — the routes the first pass could not settle

Read `../research/BRIEF.md` first: the grade RULES (one final grade = the crux, top of any range,
on one scale — `Class 1-4`, `5.x`, or `WI/AI n`; the mapping conventions; the output shape) are
unchanged. Every row here was graded at LOW confidence, or not at all, by a first researcher whose
`first_pass` (grade, evidence, sources) is attached. Your job is to do better than that, or to
confirm it with real evidence.

## The bar

- Look HARD. For each route run several searches with different spellings (peak name variants,
  "Mt"/"Mount", route name variants, the first-ascent party's names, the year). Read beyond page 1.
- Places to look: Mountain Project, SummitPost (route AND peak pages, and their trip reports),
  Peakbagger trip reports, CascadeClimbers.com trip reports, NWHikers.net trip reports, The
  Mountaineers route pages, AAJ (publications.americanalpineclub.org) first-ascent accounts,
  Alpine Institute / guide-service pages, personal climbing blogs, Google Books snippets of
  Beckey's *Cascade Alpine Guide* or *Climbing the Cascade Volcanoes*, Olympic Mountain
  Rescue's *Climber's Guide to the Olympic Mountains*, Smoot / Nelson guidebooks as quoted online.
- **Two independent sources that agree → `high`.** One clear route-specific source → `medium`.
  Only inference from the peak's general character → `low`, and say so.
- A trip report's own words count ("one move of 5.6", "never harder than 3rd class") — quote the
  short phrase in `evidence`.
- If the route as named cannot be found anywhere after a real search, say exactly what you tried
  in `note`, keep `confidence: low`, and give the record's own best grade.
- If the evidence shows the route is really a DIFFERENT, named route (identity), say so in
  `identity` — a separate pass handles those; still grade what the page describes.

## Output

Same shape as the first pass, one object per input row, same order, to the output path you are
given. Add `"searches": n` (how many searches you ran for that row) and `"changed_from_first_pass":
true|false`.
