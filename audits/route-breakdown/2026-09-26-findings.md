Each one is a research agent's reading of the sources against what the row stores. None was
written: grade, pitch count, discipline, `fa`, `beta`, `rappels` and `descent_text` are outside

Stored descent_text says descend 'the east ridge to an obvious col, then down a snow finger'; the route page says only 'East ridge and snow slopes to the North', with crampons/axe helpful but optional. Two sources, but the pitch-level lengths and grades come from one route page (whose lengths are stated as approximate) — the second source confirms features and order only.
- **`wa_gato_negro`** — grade is 5.10; sources give the route IV+ 5.10d (the P4 double corners), with the AAJ first-ascent note calling it 5.10+. beta says '11 long pitches' while pitches = 12 — both counts appear (11 in the FA report, 12 on the topo, the 12th being the often-skipped last pitch). overview calls it the 'East Face'; the AAJ first-ascent report places Whine Spire on Silver Star's west face (the route is approached from Burgundy Col on the Early Winters side). A comment offers an alternative descent: simul-climb ~300 ft of moderate class 5 to the ridge and descend the scree gully between the west peak and the true summit.
- **`wa_ellation`** — beta says the route is 'Named for FA climber Matt Alford's daughter Ella'; one web summary attributes Ella to Darin Berdinka and the trip reports say only 'one of the FA climbers' — the attribution in beta is unverified. The rappel tree off the P3 ledge is given as ~30 ft left of the P3 top on the route page and ~40 ft in another report — descent_text says 30-40 ft, consistent. length_m 244 (800 ft) vs 700 ft given in one trip report — minor.
- **`wa_garfield_mountain_infinite_bliss`** — descent_text says 'one station 10 m above the crux anchor lets you make the last pull to the base' — the source says that station lets two 60 m ropes reach the base of the CRUX PITCH, not the base of the route; the wording overstates it. - **`wa_dirty_sanchez`** — NAME: the first ascensionist has asked for the route to be called 'The Dirty' (the name used while it was being established), and the route page now uses that name; the stored name is 'Dirty Sanchez'. discipline is 'trad' but beta/overview call it an 'alpine rock route'. - **`wa_summertime`** — approach says to park at a small pullout just before the Spires Gully parking area; the crag page now says park in the largest pullout below the Hairpin Curve of SR-20 and walk down the highway, 20-30 min with a log creek crossing.
