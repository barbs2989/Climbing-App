## Where each field renders (so you can judge what a climber sees side by side)
- Header on EVERY tab: grade pill = first grade-like value of rock_grade → ice_grade → alpine_grade → grade → commitment; peak elevation = the AREA's elevation_ft; pitches; season (short form); Dist (itinerary miles if present, else dist_km); Gain (gain_ft, else itinerary gainFt sum).
- Overview: overview prose, face, TECHNICAL STATS (total ascent gain_ft, distance, high point high_point_ft, climb length length_m, pitches, crux grade from `grade`, fa), grade chips (rock_grade, commitment, alpine_grade, ice_grade, aid_grade), beta, pro_tips.
- Plan: GETTING THERE (road.*), TRAILHEAD card (approach_logistics.trailhead / trailheadDirection, Trailhead waypoint name + elev), ACCESS (access.*, permit), APPROACHES (approach_variants[] name/season/hours/distMi/gainFt/notes), APPROACH (approach), PROTECTION + RACK (detailed_rack > pro_needs > rack/gear; plus rope_type + rope_length_m, rope_note, sling_rack, alpine_draws), ROUTE BREAKDOWN (pitch_detail[] grade/length/notes) or CLIMBING ROUTE (climbing_route[]), DESCENT (the LONGER of descent / descent_text — the shorter is hidden), RAPPELS (rappel_detail table, rappels prose, rappel_count_note), Trip plan (season chip + full season text; best_season "When to go" only if no seasonal_guidance; itinerary days with gain/loss/miles/hours/pack/schedule; TOTAL row), PUBLISHED TIMES (timing.* incl. recommendedStart and sectionBreakdown), CAMPING & BIVY (bivy[] name/elev/water/permit/notes), WAYPOINTS list (name/type/elev/distMi/note/directions), GEAR & ESSENTIALS (what_to_bring), ASPECT & SUN (aspect), SEASONAL GUIDANCE (seasonal_guidance.optimalWindow + monthBreakdown, else best_season), CROWDS (crowds.*).
- Reports: CLIMATE & SEASON (climate.*).
- Safety: WeatherPanel (Trailhead/Summit waypoint names + elev), KNOWN HAZARDS (hazards + obj_haz + watch_out), SEASONAL HAZARDS (seasonal_hazards.exposure/crevasses/weather), AVALANCHE (seasonal_hazards.avalanche), BAILOUT & TURNAROUND (turnaround, bail, Bailout waypoints), cell coverage (comms), EMERGENCY (emergency.*).
- Partners: PARTNER REQUIREMENTS (partner_requirements.*).
Fields that never render on a DB route: description, data_quality, corrections, verif (one line), difficulty (radar only). A contradiction involving only non-rendered fields is low priority.
