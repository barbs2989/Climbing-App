## How it ran
- Census (`census.mjs`): 5,402 routes have 3+ pitches. Alpine is mostly covered (297/368 have a
  pitch table); trad 24/2,917, sport 8/690, ice 11/848, aid 1/429 do. 36 routes had a pitch table
  but no rappel table while their `rappels` text mentions rappels.
- Picked 40 routes: 20 well-known multipitch classics with no pitch table (`find-classics.mjs`;
  the DB's `classic`/`stars` signals are too sparse to rank by), and 20 WA routes descended by
  rappel. Context per route in `context/`.
- 9 Sonnet researcher batches (A–I) under `BRIEF.md`. WebFetch 403s on most climbing sites;
  `curl` with a browser UA reads Mountain Project (SummitPost and theCrag refuse both). Batch I
  re-did the snippet-only routes from full pages.
- Reviewer kept medium/high confidence only, applied via `enrich:apply --from` (area asserted,
  no-sources lint, all-caps lint, re-read verified). Apply files: `2026-10-23-apply-wave1..4.json`;
  the pitch tables they replaced are in `audits/route-breakdown/rollback-*.json` from this date.
