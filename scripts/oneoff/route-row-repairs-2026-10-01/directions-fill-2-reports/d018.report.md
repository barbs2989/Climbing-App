## wa_chimney_peak_the_chimney — 0 written (omitted). TWO DEFECTS
- Leg 2 (Enchanted Valley Chalet -> "Chimney Peak summit") left blank.
- DEFECT 1, wrong end point: The Chimney is a 100 ft SUBPEAK/tower on the west aspect. Its first ascent was in 1970, while the summit was first climbed in 1941. It is not the summit. The last waypoint is "Chimney Peak summit" at the GNIS summit coordinate, but the route is the tower. The summit is reached by the class 3 SW-ridge route, not by this 5.3 pitch.
- DEFECT 2, approach order: the standard SW-ridge approach leaves the East Fork Quinault trail at about 9.5 mi, at Pyrites Creek (an abandoned trail on the N side of Pyrites Creek, then NW up the valley). That is about 4 mi BEFORE the Enchanted Valley chalet (waypoint 1, 13.9 mi). The row's prose has the climbing approach leaving the trail from Enchanted Valley without saying where. If the party leaves at Pyrites Creek, the waypoint array is out of travel order: the chalet is past the turnoff.
