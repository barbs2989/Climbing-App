## wa_mount_norton_scramble: 0 written, omitted from the JSON
- Entries 0-4 already had text. Legs 5 (off-trail ascent below the east face, no pin) and 6 (summit) are left blank.
- **Defect: the route line contradicts the geography.** The "east aspect gains 3,200 ft in under a mile above the Hayes River" figure is a topographic statistic about the UPPER Hayes River valley on Norton's east side. Waypoint 5's note, the itinerary and the descent treat it as the ascent line from the Hayes River Guard Station. But the GS pin (47.8093,-123.4539) sits at the Elwha confluence, about 6 km NNW of the summit pin (47.7624,-123.4137). It is not under the east face.
- **Defect: the arithmetic does not work.** The GS is at 1,750 ft and the summit at 6,319 ft, which needs about 4,570 ft of gain. Itinerary day 3 says 3,400 ft and 4.2 mi round trip.
- Minor: the Hayes River GS elevation is 1,750 here but 2,000 in the Wilder row. Peak elevation sources give 6,397 ft against the row's 6,319 ft.

## wa_mount_steel_standard: 6 written (legs 1-6)
- Online sources agree with the row: the Marmot Lake route leaves the trail about 3 mi above Duckabush Camp, near the lake, and follows a creek into an amphitheater on the summit's west side.
- **Defect:** the primary approach_variant's `notes` say the climbers' line leaves "roughly three miles BELOW Marmot Lake". That contradicts the approach text, waypoint 2's note and the baseFinding ("if you arrive at Marmot Lake you have walked past the creek"). It also misplaces the turnoff by about 3 mi. Itinerary day 2 repeats it ("Duckabush/Marmot Lake junction about 3 miles below the lake").
- Itinerary day 3 says "leave the trail near camp... onto Steel's south/west flank". That is a looser description of the same line, not a hard conflict.
- Waypoint order: the order is TH → camps → Marmot Lake (base camp) → amphitheater. Leg 5 says to "drop back down the trail" from camp, which is a real base-camp day and is how waypoint 5's own note describes it. It is not a repair of a broken array. Flagging it so you can judge.

- Legs 1-3 follow the Elwha River Trail, with mileages from the row and the land-manager campsite list.
- Legs 4-6 come only from the row's approach prose (brush and forest, then heather benches, then the south-face scree, then the south ridge). Wilder's listed easiest route is Class 2 via the south ridge.

## wa_sundial_northeast: 7 written (legs 1-7)
- I checked the order against a trail description: Royal Lake (7.2 mi) → Shelter Rock (overhang on the left, then a footlog) → Arrowhead Meadow → an unbridged stream → Upper Royal Basin (about 8 mi, tarns to the west). The order is correct.
- **Defect:** Shelter Rock's elevation is 4,900 ft. It is above Royal Lake (5,107 ft), so that number is wrong.
- Note: the gully pin (47.8357,-123.2221) is about 1.5 km NORTH of the tarns pin. It is also north of Royal Lake's latitude, NE of the summit. That fits a NE gully on the north (climber's-right) end of the Needles. But it means the party walks back north from the tarns to the gully, and a ski trip report started the couloir from the "middle portion" of the basin. The tarns waypoint may be a detour on this line. I did not treat it as broken, and I worded leg 6 without a compass bearing.

- Minor: Flapjack Lakes' elevation is 3,900 in the waypoint, 3,850 in the itinerary and ~4,000 in the approach. The junction is 3.4 mi in the row; another trail description gives 3.7 mi.
