# Phase 3 Tier 3: Remote WA Sport Climbing Hazard Research

**Status**: INITIATED 2026-07-28  
**Target**: Dry Falls + Tri-Cities area sport climbing  
**Scope**: 15-25 hazards per route  
**Focus**: Remote-sport-hazards (rescue-access-difficult, communication-unreliable, heat-exposure-desert, rattlesnake-season)

## Research Areas

### 1. Dry Falls Area
- **dry-falls-main**: Primary climbing wall complex
- **dry-falls-upper**: Upper basin secondary walls
- **Classification**: Desert sport climbing, basalt columnar formations
- **Access**: Remote, limited cell reception
- **Season**: Spring/Fall (heat hazard May-Sep)
- **Rock Type**: Pillar basalt, exfoliation risk

### 2. Tri-Cities Area  
- **tri-cities-crags**: Pasco/Richland/Kennewick vicinity
- **Classification**: Basalt sport climbing, reservoir-adjacent
- **Access**: Limited rescue infrastructure
- **Season**: Year-round, heat exposure most hazardous Jun-Sep
- **Rock Type**: Columnar basalt, quality variable

### 3. Remote WA Basalt Sport
- **remote-wa-basalt-sport**: Other outlying basalt formations
- **Geographic**: Eastern WA Columbia River Plateau
- **Classification**: Developing areas, less-established routes
- **Access**: Very limited emergency services

## Hazard Categories (Target 15-25 per route)

### Remote/Access Hazards
- [ ] Rescue access difficult (distance, terrain, helicopter unfeasible)
- [ ] Emergency services >30 mins away
- [ ] Cell reception unreliable/nonexistent
- [ ] No trailhead facilities
- [ ] Multiple approach options (navigation risk)
- [ ] No established emergency protocols

### Environmental Hazards
- [ ] Heat exposure (ambient 95-110F Jun-Sep)
- [ ] Dehydration risk (sparse water sources)
- [ ] Sun reflection/albedo hazard (rock/sand)
- [ ] Lightning exposure (isolated rocks)
- [ ] Weather volatility (afternoon wind gusts)
- [ ] Dust/sand storms (visibility, respiratory)

### Rock Quality Hazards
- [ ] Pillar basalt exfoliation (sheets detach)
- [ ] Weathered columnar jointing (gaps trap limbs)
- [ ] Friable sections (upper pitches)
- [ ] Loose rock abundance (previous parties)
- [ ] Bolt quality issues (corrosion/missing hangers)
- [ ] Pre-bolting consistency variable
- [ ] No protection for certain pitches

### Crowd/Traffic Hazards
- [ ] Popular bolts (concentrated wear, foot traffic)
- [ ] Simultaneous parties on same wall (not common)
- [ ] Belay platform crowding
- [ ] First-bolt danger (exposure before pro)
- [ ] Rappel line conflicts

### Wildlife Hazards
- [ ] Rattlesnakes (Jun-Oct, dawn/dusk)
- [ ] Scorpions (nocturnal, remote areas)
- [ ] Bees/wasps (nesting in rock)
- [ ] Coyotes (rare but present)

### Technical Route Hazards
- [ ] Runout sections (minimal bolts)
- [ ] Committing finishes (no bail option)
- [ ] Anchor quality variable (slings vs. bolts)
- [ ] Descent scrambling hazard
- [ ] No established retreat options

## Data Quality Requirements

Each route must include:
- **Specific location**: Which wall, sector, approach
- **Seasonal context**: When hazards peak
- **Incident history**: Documented accidents if known
- **Mitigation**: Specific actions to reduce risk
- **Source verification**: 2+ independent sources minimum
